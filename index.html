How pickup works

No delivery, no waiting around. We cook to your chosen time so it is hot when you walk in.

  1. Build your order

    Add dishes from the menu and choose sizes. Your total updates as you go.

  2. Choose a date and time

    Pick any open day this week and a 15-minute collection slot. Today's earliest slot is 25 minutes from now.

  3. Collect at the counter

    Give your name at 18 Ember Lane. Pay when you collect by card or cash.

About us

Family kitchen, wood-fired since 2019

Crust & Ember started with one brick oven and a simple rule: make the dough properly and cook everything to order. Our pizzas are stretched by hand from dough rested for 48 hours, our burger patties are ground in-house every morning, and our ramen broth simmers for twelve hours.

We are a small team, and we would rather do a short menu well than a long one badly. Everything you order here is cooked when you ask for it, then handed over the counter.
